## 2. Inviolable Directives for Agents

1. **Directive G-01 (Strict Double-Entry Enforcement)**:
   - All financial vouchers (Journal Vouchers, Cash Vouchers, Bank Payments) MUST enforce strict mathematical balance (`debit == credit`) prior to database persistence.
2. **Directive G-02 (Statutory Book Generation)**:
   - Day Book, Cash Book, Bank Book, and General Ledger reports MUST generate on demand with real-time running balances and direct transaction drill-down.
3. **Directive G-03 (Multi-Board Assessment Parity)**:
   - Exam evaluation engines MUST support both CBSE/CCE (Scholastic + Co-Scholastic descriptive indicators) and ICSE (Group I, II, III weightages with external board and internal coursework splits).
4. **Directive G-04 (Algorithmic Question Paper Generation)**:
   - Automated paper compilation MUST draw questions from the central repository matching specified Bloom's taxonomy difficulty ratios (Easy 30%, Moderate 50%, Challenging 20%) and section mark distributions.
5. **Directive G-05 (Payroll Incentive Integrity)**:
   - Monthly payroll generation MUST compute salary components, state professional tax slabs, approved leaves, and festival/performance bonuses deterministically.
6. **Directive G-06 (Perimeter Gate Attendance Enforcement)**:
   - Student Late Arrival and Early Departure gate events MUST generate immediate timestamped passes and dispatch automated parental alerts.
7. **Directive G-07 (Deleted Receipt Forensic Auditing)**:
   - Any cancellation or deletion of an advance or standard fee receipt MUST append an immutable audit record containing operator ID, timestamp, and mandatory cancellation justification.
8. **Directive G-08 (Strict Zero Emoji Directive)**:
   - Zero emojis across all financial statements, ledger entries, exam marksheets, payslips, gate passes, and inventory orders. Use Google Material Symbols Outlined exclusively.
