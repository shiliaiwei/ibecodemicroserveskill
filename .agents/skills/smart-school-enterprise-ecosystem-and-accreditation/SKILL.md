## 2. Inviolable Directives for Agents

1. **Directive E-01 (Multi-Curriculum Polymorphism)**: Transcripts and marksheet generators MUST adapt dynamically to the enrolled student's curriculum track (IB 1-7 criterion scales, Cambridge A*-G / 9-1 scales, American 4.0 GPA, or national percentage bands).
2. **Directive E-02 (CIS Child Safeguarding Isolation)**: Pastoral wellbeing and child safeguarding records MUST be encrypted with dedicated keys and restricted strictly to designated Child Protection Officers (CPOs) and Counselors. Regular staff and teachers MUST NOT have access.
3. **Directive E-03 (Lightweight Embeddable Admissions)**: The public website enrollment widget MUST load in under 200ms, maintain zero external runtime dependencies, and communicate via CORS-secured REST endpoints.
4. **Directive E-04 (Tamper-Evident Audit Logging)**: In compliance with CIS accreditation standards, all modifications to grades, student personal records, fee invoices, and attendance records MUST append immutable audit log entries.
5. **Directive E-05 (Identity SSO SAML 2.0 / OIDC)**: Enterprise school authentication MUST support seamless single-sign-on integration with Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), and Okta.
6. **Directive E-06 (Strict Zero Emoji Directive)**: Zero emojis across all accreditation audit logs, developer API documentation, student transcripts, and customer help guides. Use Google Material Symbols Outlined exclusively.
