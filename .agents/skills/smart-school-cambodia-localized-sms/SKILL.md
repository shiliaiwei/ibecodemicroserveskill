## 2. Inviolable Directives for Agents

1. **Directive K-01 (Strict What, Why, Where & How Structuring)**:
   - Every module, component, and technical specification MUST articulate:
     - **WHAT TO HAVE**: The concrete UI components, entities, endpoints, and data contracts.
     - **WHY IT EXISTS**: The pedagogical, regulatory (MoEYS), or administrative justification.
     - **WHERE IT IS PLACED**: The exact URL routes, layout archetypes, and database tables.
     - **HOW IT WORKS**: The end-to-end data lifecycle, user flows, and state machine transitions.
2. **Directive K-02 (Dual-Currency KHR & USD Invoicing)**:
   - All tuition invoices, payment receipts, fee summaries, and financial reports MUST compute and display dual-currency balances in Cambodian Riel (`៛` KHR) and US Dollar (`$`). Real-time National Bank of Cambodia (NBC) exchange rates MUST be supported.
3. **Directive K-03 (National Payment Gateway Ingress)**:
   - Payment checkout flows in Cambodia MUST prioritize the National Bank of Cambodia Bakong KHQR standard, ABA PayWay mobile deep-linking, and Wing Bank counter cash codes.
4. **Directive K-04 (Khmer Typography & Grapheme Integrity)**:
   - All Khmer UI text MUST render in Google Sans Khmer (`font-khmer`).
   - Official diplomas, completion certificates, and institutional marksheet titles MUST render in Moul (`font-moul`).
   - Zero word breakage MUST be enforced using zero-width spaces (`\u200B`) between word tokens to prevent broken syllables.
5. **Directive K-05 (4-Tier Institutional Specialization)**:
   - **Schools**: K-12 MoEYS curriculum, BacII graduation tracking, and parent attendance alerts.
   - **Colleges**: Undergrad & TVET credits, workshop safety tracking, and internship monitoring.
   - **Institutes**: Modular fast-track certifications, evening/weekend shift scheduling, and corporate billing.
   - **Universities**: Multi-faculty governance, CATS credit transfers, thesis defense milestones, and degree audits.
6. **Directive K-06 (City Distributor Network Operations)**:
   - System onboarding workflows MUST support distributed training and localization desks for Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Battambang, Sihanoukville, and Kampong Cham.
7. **Directive K-07 (Sub-45s Classroom Attendance & Mobile Parity)**:
   - Classroom roll-calls MUST execute in under 45 seconds per section with immediate offline SQLite sync and mobile responsiveness.
8. **Directive K-08 (Strict Zero Emoji Directive)**:
   - Zero emojis across all Cambodian ministry marksheets, student dossiers, Bakong receipts, and system documentation. Use Google Material Symbols Outlined exclusively.
